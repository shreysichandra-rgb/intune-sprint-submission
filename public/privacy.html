InTune Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

InTune helps you remember reported body changes and keep track of your existing plans, food lists and updates you type yourself. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Who operates InTune

InTune is operated by Shreysi Chandra, operating InTune. For privacy questions, contact shreysi@spunky.media.

Information we collect and store

When you use InTune through WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, messages, captions, uploaded files, message references and WhatsApp message IDs. Using InTune implies permission to retain information you share; you do not need to approve every clear update. Reminder permission and paid access are separate. You can tell us not to save particular information, withdraw saving, or request deletion.

We store information needed to maintain your record, including:

  • Your phone number, internal user identifier, tracking settings and tracking state.
  • Messages saved as reported occurrences, corrections, plan proposals, confirmed plans, completion updates or unresolved questions.
  • Dates, times, counts, confirmation states and the server’s message-received time.
  • Message identifiers and processing states used to prevent duplicate counting, usage-limit information and deletion markers.
  • Any stored queued output, including summary text.

Messages about symptoms or health plans may contain sensitive health information. Please send only information you want InTune to process.

Health tracking accepts typed text only. We separately keep a private debugging record of incoming messages and captions, uploaded screenshots, photos, documents, audio and other media, replies, timestamps, references and processing/delivery outcomes. This includes unsupported uploads and unpaid or expired interactions. The founder can privately review these records to understand issues. Upload retention does not enable medical interpretation, voice transcription or automatic health extraction. We redact recognized credentials and private checkout tokens from text copies. Binary uploads are private original files; we cannot promise automatic redaction of credentials shown inside an image or other binary file.

Why we use this information

We use it to interpret your messages, maintain and correct your records, calculate progress, retrieve your summaries, answer you and enforce your control commands and usage limits, and investigate conversation and delivery issues.

Service providers

  • Meta/WhatsApp carries your incoming messages and our replies. We send your recipient phone number and reply text through the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Convex hosts InTune’s pages, backend, database and private uploaded files.
  • OpenAI interprets messages that require AI processing. The request includes your message, tracked changes, confirmed plan activity and target when applicable, and bounded relevant conversation and recent-record context. Complete transcripts and uploads are not routinely sent to AI. The application does not include your phone number in that AI prompt.

AI message-history saving is disabled in the application. This does not establish that service providers retain no data. Their processing and retention are governed by their terms and account settings; OpenAI describes its API data controls.

Storage and deletion

Saved health records and debugging records remain until you ask us to forget or delete them. Expiry stops new health tracking; it does not erase existing records.

Sending Stop closes the current tracking job while keeping saved records available. Sending Delete my pilot data removes your stored health records, plans, completion records, unresolved items, references and stored output copies from InTune’s application database, including retained conversation text, captions and uploaded files. Large debugging histories become unavailable immediately while physical deletion finishes in bounded batches. In-flight uploads cannot restore deleted content.

The confirmation says:

Your health records have been deleted. Your phone number and service records remain. All proactive messages are stopped.

That deletion leaves your user record, including your phone number, internal identifiers, stopped state and deletion timestamp, along with message-processing records, usage-limit records and deletion markers.

The command does not delete your WhatsApp chat or establish deletion of copies held in service-provider logs or backups.

Send Stop saving to withdraw saving and clear debugging copies and temporary conversation context while keeping existing validated health records available. Send Save again to resume saving; paid access and reminder permission are unchanged. A Forget request also clears debugging copies that might contain the forgotten detail. Private manual exports and external provider backups are outside automatic application deletion.

Website storage

The web test page stores access tokens for its fictional test users in your browser’s local storage so that the test session can survive a reload.
