InTune Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 10 October 2026

InTune is operated by Shreysi Chandra, operating InTune. For deletion questions, contact shreysi@spunky.media.

Delete your health records

  1. Open your WhatsApp conversation with InTune using the number you used to send your records.
  2. Send exactly: Delete my pilot data
  3. Look for this confirmation:
Your health records have been deleted. Your phone number and service records remain. All proactive messages are stopped.

If you do not receive confirmation, contact shreysi@spunky.media. Do not assume deletion succeeded.

What the command deletes

It removes your reported occurrences, correction text held in those records, unresolved messages, confirmed and pending plans, completion records, references, and stored queued or dropped output—including stored summary text—from InTune’s application database. It also deletes retained conversation text, captions and uploaded files. These debugging copies become unavailable immediately; large histories are physically deleted in bounded batches. In-flight uploads cannot recreate deleted files.

What remains

Your user record, including your phone number, internal identifiers, stopped state and deletion timestamp, remains. Message-processing records, usage-limit records and deletion markers also remain.

The command does not delete messages from your WhatsApp chat or guarantee removal from service-provider logs or backups.

Stop is different

Sending Stop ends the current tracking job but preserves saved records. Use Delete my pilot data when you want the health records removed.

Withdraw saving

Send Stop saving to withdraw saving and clear debugging copies and temporary context while keeping existing validated health records. Send Save again to resume; payment and reminder permission do not change. Forgetting a detail conservatively clears debugging copies that might contain it. Private manual exports and external provider copies are outside automatic application deletion.
