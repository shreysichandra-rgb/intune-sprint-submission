A few things to know.

Can I send a photo, PDF or voice note?

Text only for health plans right now. Paste the plan as text. Unsupported uploads are retained separately for debugging; they aren’t interpreted as health records.

What gets kept?

Your messages, saved details and debugging attachments stay until you ask for deletion. InTune operators may review them to understand and fix problems. Relevant text passes through WhatsApp/Meta, Convex and OpenAI.

Deleting InTune’s copies does not remove messages already delivered in WhatsApp or third-party service records.

Will InTune remind me automatically?

Reminders need your request and a clear time. Real delivery for this new version is still being checked. The launch page will say what is available after that check. InTune doesn’t book appointments, place orders or recommend treatment.
