# InTune pilot: approved safety review

Source: clinician review supplied by the operator in this session. Approval: “Approved as written: Yes”; “Changes done”. Review date as supplied: 10/07/2026. Clinician identity is kept outside the repository. No patient information is included.

## Approved trigger categories

- chest pain or chest pressure
- shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
- fainting or loss of consciousness
- sudden weakness/numbness, facial droop, or difficulty speaking
- headache
- Concerning bleeding: heavy, unusually heavy, uncontrolled, or clearly concerning bleeding
- allergic reaction
- Skin rash
- seizure
- abdominal/pelvic pain
- fever
- suicidal thoughts, self-harm intent, or immediate danger to self
- Urinary symptoms
- Diarrhoea

Operator-supplied clinician clarification (7 October 2026): normal menstrual/cycle changes — period started today, late/missed/early periods, menstrual bleeding and ordinary spotting — stay in normal tracking. Low mood, anxiety, crying and feeling overwhelmed are not mental-health triggers without actual suicidal thoughts, self-harm intent or immediate danger to self.

No additional severity thresholds, duration thresholds, diagnosis or treatment rules were supplied. Do not add any.

## Exact approved medical safety response

I want to pause the usual tracking here because what you’ve described may need medical attention.
Please seek urgent medical care now or contact your local emergency service.

## Exact approved mental-health / immediate-safety response

I’m concerned about what you’ve just told me. Please contact emergency services or a local crisis service
I’m not able to provide emergency or crisis care.

## Approved exclusions from InTune's behaviour

Do not diagnose the condition, interpret test results, say the symptom is probably harmless, estimate severity, suggest medication or dosage changes, or delay care by continuing the normal symptom-tracking conversation.

## Approved return rule: Option B

Before resuming tracking, InTune asks:

“Are you okay to continue with normal tracking now?”

Option A (automatic user-led return after an in-scope message) was not selected. Normal text alone must not clear the safety pause. The implementation requires an explicit affirmative answer after the above question; a new safety trigger interrupts that check. The affirmative answer itself does not log a symptom or confirm a plan.
